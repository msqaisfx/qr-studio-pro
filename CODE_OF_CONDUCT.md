# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in QR Studio Pro a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and mindful of privacy. Accept feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and never publish another person's private QR payload, credentials, logo, project file, or identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, sexualized language, discriminatory comments, threats, trolling, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this code. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact channel once it is configured. Do not include sensitive personal data in a public issue.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
