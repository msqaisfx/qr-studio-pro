- One semantic `main` landmark and visible heading hierarchy found.
- 27 visible interactive controls checked; 0 unnamed controls and 0 unlabeled form fields.
- 3 live regions available for status feedback.
- Arabic starts with `lang=ar`, `dir=rtl`; language toggle produced `lang=en`, `dir=ltr`.

- 375/768/1280/1920 widths had no horizontal overflow.

Limitations: no VoiceOver/NVDA session, human keyboard-only traversal, or full WCAG conformance certification was performed. QR contrast guidance is advisory and is not presented as WCAG certification for QR readability.
